Privacy Policy

"RouteTracker" values your privacy. This privacy policy explains how the app collects, uses, and protects your data.

1. Information We Collect

1-1. Location Data

RouteTracker collects location data to display your current position and track your activity routes. This information is collected only with your explicit consent.

The collected location data is used for the following purposes:

  • Displaying current location and routes
  • Analyzing and saving activity statistics

This data is not shared with third parties, and users can change location permission settings at any time within the app settings.

1-2. Fitness Data

To analyze your real-time workout intensity and provide accurate, personalized records, RouteTracker collects fitness information such as heart rate, gender, age, height, and weight. This information is collected only with your explicit consent.

The collected information is used for the following purposes:

  • Monitoring real-time workout intensity
  • Calculating accurate calorie burn and analyzing workout effectiveness

This data is not shared with third parties, and you can change your permission settings and edit your data at any time within the app.

1-3. Google Drive Data

The app does not directly collect or store your data on its own servers. However, it uses the Google Drive API to allow access to and management of files you've explicitly approved. Access is limited strictly to the scope you've authorized.

2. Use of Data

The app uses data for the following purposes:

  • Reading, writing, and modifying files in your Google Drive upon your request
  • Accessing file lists and metadata

The app does not store any data on its own servers. All operations are confined to your personal Google Drive account.

3. Data Sharing

The app does not share your data with third parties. Your data is managed according to Google's privacy policy.

4. Data Security

The app uses secure communication protocols provided by the Google Drive API to protect data transmission. No data is stored or transmitted externally.

5. User Permissions

The app accesses data only with your explicit permission through the Google Drive API. You can revoke access at any time via your Google account settings.

6. Data Retention & Destruction

This app ('RouteTracker') does not store user data on separate servers; all information (workout records, user settings, physical information, etc.) is stored securely only on your personal device.

Therefore, if you delete the app from your device, all data associated with the app will be permanently deleted along with it.

Additionally, you can immediately delete all of the app's information by clearing the app's data in your operating system's settings app.

7. Third-Party Services

The app uses the Google Drive API and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

This privacy policy remains effective while you use the app and may be updated as necessary.
