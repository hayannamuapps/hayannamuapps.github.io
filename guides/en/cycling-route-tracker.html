What a cycling route tracker should answer

Compare the complete ride workflow, not only a live speed screen.

RouteTracker is an iPhone and Android cycling route tracker.

It records rides with route maps, speed or pace metrics, elevation, GPX import and export, notes, activity history, optional Google Drive backup, and Pro tools for repeated-ride analysis.

RouteTracker cycling features

These are the product facts behind the cycling route tracker use case.

Ride maps and core metrics

Record a cycling route with distance, time, speed, elevation, calendar history, and notes for later review.

GPX-compatible ride records

Import a GPX route, record the real ride while using it as a map reference, and export the completed activity as GPX.

Repeated ride analysis

Pro adds route planning, split comparison, growth charts, and Local AI reference insights for repeated routes.

Device-first storage

Core recording can start without account sign-up. Activity data stays on the device until you choose backup.

Check the limits

GPS accuracy and battery use depend on the phone, permissions, power settings, terrain, and signal environment.

RouteTracker focuses on personal route records. It is not a social feed, club, leaderboard, rescue service, or safety guarantee.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers for people comparing cycling route trackers.

Can RouteTracker record cycling routes?

Yes. RouteTracker records cycling routes on iPhone and Android with maps, distance, time, speed, elevation, calendar history, GPX files, and notes.

Does RouteTracker support GPX for cycling routes?

Yes. Import a GPX route, view it on the map while recording, and export a completed cycling activity as a GPX file.

Are repeated-ride comparisons and growth charts free?

No. Repeated-route analysis, split comparison, growth charts, and Local AI insights are RouteTracker Pro features.

Where are cycling activity records stored?

Core activity records stay on the device until you choose backup. Plus and Pro support user-approved Google Drive snapshots.
