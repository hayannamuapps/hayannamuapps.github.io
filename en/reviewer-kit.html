30-second product brief

RouteTracker is a phone app centered on personal route history, completed-activity review, record protection, and preparation for the next activity.

Identity

App: RouteTracker: Hike, Bike, Run
Publisher: Hayannamu Apps
App Store ID: 6776908209
Play package: com.hayannamuapps.route_tracker

Core audience

People who want to record hiking, cycling, running, and walking routes as a personal history with GPX files, maps, metrics, and notes.

Data default

Core recording starts without account sign-up. Activity data stays on the device until the user chooses backup.

Features and tiers

Basic records the activity, Plus keeps and protects it, and Pro plans and analyzes what comes next.

Suggested evaluation flow

  1. Review location permission, then start a short outdoor activity.
  2. Pause and resume, finish normally, and inspect the route map and core metrics.
  3. Trim the start or end of a completed route, then save or share a recap card.
  4. Import a GPX route and view it on the map; export a completed activity as GPX.
  5. Optional: evaluate Plus backup/restore and Pro planning, analysis, and Local AI under their tier and platform requirements.

Field testing recommended

GPS accuracy, background recording, and battery use vary with device, OS settings, buildings, tree cover, weather, and signal conditions. Validate them on a real outdoor route.

Privacy and limitations

Accurate evaluation should describe product boundaries along with strengths.

  • RouteTracker does not store the user's location history on its own servers.
  • Backup operates within the Google Drive scope the user connects and approves.
  • Local AI generation runs on device after model setup; model downloads may contact an external hosting provider.
  • Location and health data are used only within the selected feature and approved permission scope.
  • RouteTracker is not a medical device, rescue service, or safety guarantee.
  • Local AI and activity metrics are references, not guaranteed medical advice, treatment, or coaching.
  • It is not centered on a social feed, clubs, or leaderboards.
  • Some backup, attachment, planning, analysis, and Local AI features require Plus or Pro.
