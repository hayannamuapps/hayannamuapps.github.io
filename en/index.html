Manage your records your way.

RouteTracker stores activity data on your device until you choose to back it up. When backup is enabled, snapshots are saved to the Google Drive account you connect.

  • Location tracking Explicit permission is required to show your current location and record activity routes.
  • Google Drive For Plus and higher tiers, RouteTracker accesses only the user-approved scope needed for backup and restore.
  • Health data For Pro features, user-approved heart rate data from Apple Health or Health Connect can be reflected in activity analysis.
  • Local AI Pro Local AI insights run on device, while downloading model files may contact model hosting providers such as Hugging Face.

Key facts to review before installing

A concise summary of availability, data handling, and subscription tiers.

What is RouteTracker?

RouteTracker is a mobile app for recording hiking, cycling, running, and walking routes, then reviewing completed activities with maps, calendar history, core stats, GPX files, and notes.

Where is RouteTracker currently available?

RouteTracker is available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android on Google Play.

Where is activity data stored?

Activity data stays on the device unless the user chooses backup. When backup is enabled, RouteTracker stores and restores snapshots within the user-approved Google Drive scope.

How are Plus and Pro different?

Plus focuses on keeping and protecting activity history with ad removal, file attachments, and Google Drive backup and restore. Pro includes Plus and adds route planning, advanced maps, deeper analysis, and Local AI insights.
