Data & backup

Why RouteTracker separates device-first records from Google Drive backup

Starting a route record without first requiring a cloud account and protecting records against device loss are different problems. RouteTracker separates local recording from an optional backup choice.

HayanNamuApps product teamPublished: 2026-07-22Updated: 2026-07-22

What device-first means

Core activity records stay on the device unless the user chooses backup. A dedicated RouteTracker account is not required before starting core recording.

This does not mean a backup is unnecessary. It means the default storage location and the point of external transfer remain distinct choices. App deletion, device damage, or loss still requires a separate protection plan.

The Google Drive backup boundary

Google Drive backup and restore are Plus and Pro features. After the user connects a Google account and approves the required scope, RouteTracker can save or restore an activity-record snapshot within that Drive scope.

  • This is not continuous synchronization through a RouteTracker-owned cloud account.
  • Check the result of a manual or configured automatic backup and its latest successful state.
  • Before restore, verify the destination device and account and preserve important current records separately.

A practical device-migration check

  1. On the old device, confirm that recent activities are complete and open correctly in the list and detail view.
  2. Run backup with the intended Google account and confirm success.
  3. Keep especially important activities separately as GPX when practical.
  4. On the new device, verify the same account and intended backup before restoring.
  5. After restore, sample the activity count, recent records, maps, and important attachments.

Limits to check before use

  • Google Drive backup and restore depend on Plus or Pro access, user authentication and approval, network connectivity, and Google Drive availability.
  • A backup is a point-in-time snapshot. Changes after the last successful backup may be absent until another backup succeeds.
  • Restore is not an unconditional guarantee against data loss. Confirm success and keep important GPX files separately before device replacement or app removal.
